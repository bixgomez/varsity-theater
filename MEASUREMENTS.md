# Projection booth measurements

All measurements are in inches.  Unless noted otherwise, horizontal positions are measured from the south wall, depths from the west (window) wall, and heights from the floor.

## Room

- Width (south to north): 179
- Depth (west to east): 128
- Height: 87 (88 at the east wall; we use 87)

## West wall (auditorium side)

- **Conduit ledge:** runs the full width; 32.5 to 39 high; 3.5 deep.
- **Vertical conduit:** at the south end; 6.5 wide, 3.25 deep; runs up from the ledge.
- **Control box:** a wedge-shaped switch panel sitting on the ledge; 65 to 101 from south; 39 to 50.5 high; 7 deep at the base, tapering to 3.5 deep from 3 above the base.  The 36 width is an estimate.

## East wall (door side)

- **Door opening:** 25 to 61 from south; 80.5 tall.  The door is hinged on the south side and opens into the booth.
- **Electrical closet:** in the southeast corner; 0 to 21 from south; runs 50 in from the east wall; floor to ceiling.
- **Rewind table:** right next to the door on its north side, about 63 to 123.5 from south; 2 out from the east wall; 22 deep; 38.5 tall; partly open underneath.

## Ceiling

- **Duct:** runs the full width; 46 to 58 out from the east wall; 12 wide, 5.5 tall.
- **Exhaust box:** 45.5 to 54 out from the west wall; 8.5 by 8.5; hangs 9.5 below the ceiling.

## Equipment

### Barco SP4K-12C (digital projector)

From Barco's spec sheet:

- **Size (W × L × H):** 27.95 × 42.13 × 21.54, including feet; 44.55 long with the B-lensholder
- **Weight:** 198.5 lb
- **Exhaust airflow:** 300 CFM
- **Heat load:** 4600 BTU/h at max power
- **Power:** 200 to 240V, 16A, single or three phase
- **Lens position:** not on the spec sheet; check the installation manual's dimension drawing

The plan is to put the digital projector in the center, with a 35mm projector on each side.  Each 35mm projector needs threading room on its right side.
